01The founder

Meet
Naitik.

NaitikGurnani
Grade 9Student
CanadaBased in

Born in India and now living in Canada, Naitik started DRIVEN at the beginning of 2026 because he wanted to build something of his own.

He wanted clothing with a meaning behind it, beyond putting a logo on a shirt. While balancing Grade 9, he has been learning the parts of building a brand firsthand: design, suppliers, budgeting, an online store, and marketing.

Most of what he knows about business has come from actually working on DRIVEN and figuring things out as he goes.

“You don’t have to be successful to start. You just have to be driven.”Naitik Gurnani

Source: Shaun Santhosh’s in-person conversation with Naitik Gurnani.

05The journey

Building it
as he goes.

Naitik is building DRIVEN while he’s in school. He hasn’t invested in other businesses; this is where his focus is right now. DRIVEN is still new, so it hasn’t received any awards or public customer reviews yet.

Early 2026

The start

Naitik begins DRIVEN to turn a personal idea into a real brand.

In progress

Learning by doing

Design, suppliers, budgeting, store setup, and marketing become part of the work.

Summer 2026

Friends started wearing it

Seeing people around him in DRIVEN clothing made the brand feel real, and others began asking about it.

One Specific Challenge

“One of the biggest challenges I had to overcome was figuring out how to get good-quality clothing without spending more money than I could afford.

Since DRIVEN is a new business and I have a limited budget, I couldn’t just keep ordering samples until I found the perfect one. I had to compare different suppliers, look at different materials and fits, and decide what was actually worth spending money on.

It took some trial and error, but it taught me that running a business isn’t always about getting things right the first time. Sometimes you have to test things, make mistakes, learn from them, and keep going.”

— Naitik Gurnani
+Impact on the Community

“Even though DRIVEN is still a small brand, it has started to become something that my friends and people around me can be involved in.

During the summer, some of my friends wore DRIVEN clothing just for fun and supported the brand. It was cool seeing people I know actually wearing something that I created. It also helped other people notice the brand and ask about it.

For me, that showed that DRIVEN can be more than just a clothing brand. It can be something that brings people together and gives people something they can be proud to wear.”

— Naitik Gurnani

Naitik’s words above are quoted exactly from his written follow-up after the in-person conversation.
